Privacy Policy — GharTasalli

Effective date: 10 October 2026

What this app is

GharTasalli is a home-services booking app for Pakistan. The app shows our public website (service list, booking fee, FAQs) and lets you contact us on WhatsApp to book a service.

Data we collect

Through the app itself, we collect nothing. The app creates no user accounts and stores no personal data on your device or our servers.

If you message us on WhatsApp to book a service, you share details such as your name, address and phone number directly in the chat so we can send a worker to your home. We use that information only to arrange your booking and do not sell or share it.

WhatsApp chats are governed by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Payments

The Rs 150 booking fee is paid by you directly via Easypaisa/JazzCash. We do not process or store any payment credentials.

Contact

For any privacy question, message us on the WhatsApp number shown on our website: https://gharthak312-droid.github.io/ghartasalli/
